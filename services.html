FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, but most websites take 3–6 weeks and apps 8–14 weeks. After our first call we'll give you a clear timeline with milestones.

Do you work with startups as well as larger companies?

Yes. We work with founders building their first product and with established businesses scaling existing systems. We tailor the engagement to your size and stage.

Will I own the code and designs?

Absolutely. On completion, all source code, designs and assets are yours. We hand everything over with documentation.

Yes — we offer ongoing maintenance, monitoring and improvement plans so your product stays fast, secure and up to date.
